I am a cultural sociologist with broad research interests in race and ethnicity, gender and sexuality, and food studies. I am a PhD Candidate at the University of California, Irvine. I am currently working on my dissertation entitled What We Eat, Who We Are: Food, Identity, and Community Among Asian Americans which investigates the role of food and foodways in identity maintenance and formation for Asian Americans, with a focus on how these processes differ across life stages and gender.

In addition to writing academic books and articles, I also co-edit [The Recipes Project](https://recipes.hypotheses.org), a quarterly online publication devoted to recipe studies of all kinds.
